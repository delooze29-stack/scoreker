Doughty's Links

  • Aim: drag the ring, or swipe the course left and right. Swipe up and down to change distance.
  • Power: grab your ball and pull it down. Past 100% is extra power, but the green zone shrinks.
  • Accuracy: let go when the needle is in the green. The harder you pull, the faster it swings. A yellow marker on the course shows how far that power will send the ball, before wind and slopes.
  • Spin: tap the ball bottom-left. Top for topspin, bottom for backspin, left side to draw and right side to fade.
  • Green view: on approach shots, tap Green view (or press G) for a close-up of the green. The yellow dots show how the ball should roll.
  • Slopes and wind: the ball rolls with the ground, so read the grid and drifting dots on the greens. The flag, trees and dust show the wind.
