Contact reference

Overlap = 1 − sin(cut angle). Half-ball is a 30° cut; thinner than ⅛ ball is past 60°. Centre ball is a stun shot, so the white leaves at 90° to the object ball's path. Top makes it follow through, screw pulls it back.

Off a cushion, side changes the rebound. Side spinning the same way the ball runs along the cushion (running side) widens the angle; the other side (check side) narrows it. Squirt and swerve are left out, so side doesn't move your first contact.
